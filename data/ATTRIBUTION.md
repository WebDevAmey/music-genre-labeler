`musiccaps_sample.csv` is a random sample (200 rows, `random_state=7`) of the
**MusicCaps** dataset by Google (Agostinelli et al., 2023, "MusicLM: Generating
Music From Text"), licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Columns kept: `ytid@start_s` as `id`,
`caption` as `text`. This sample is shared under the same license.
